Work problems and leaving a job in Japan

Before resigning, check your employment contract, work rules, paid leave balance, final payday and next job start date.

When should I resign?

There is no universally best resignation date. Check your employment status, work rules, payroll closing date, social insurance qualification-loss date and your next employer's start date. Ask HR or your insurer before deciding.

Can I use paid leave before resigning?

Check your remaining paid leave and discuss the schedule with your employer. Keep written records of requests and replies. For advice, contact the Labour Standards Inspection Office or a General Labour Consultation Corner.

My employer has not paid wages or overtime

Keep payslips, time records, work schedules and messages. Ask the employer in writing for payment details. If unresolved, consult the Labour Standards Inspection Office.

I have not received my separation documents

Ask the employer when the documents will be issued. If you need to apply for unemployment benefits and documents are delayed, contact Hello Work for guidance.
