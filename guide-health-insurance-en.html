Health insurance and pension after leaving a job

If you leave a job in Japan, you may need to arrange health insurance and pension coverage. Your options depend on your next job and family situation.

1. Choose your health insurance

Common options are National Health Insurance through your municipality, voluntary continuation of your previous employee health insurance, or coverage as a dependent of a family member. Check eligibility and premiums before deciding.

2. Check the deadlines

Municipal National Health Insurance procedures are generally due within 14 days of the relevant event. Voluntary continuation usually requires an application within 20 days after loss of eligibility and has other eligibility conditions. Confirm the exact deadline with the insurer or municipality.

3. Check your pension status

If you are not joining another employer's Employees' Pension, you may need to switch to National Pension Category I. If you become a dependent spouse, different rules may apply. Contact your municipal office or Japan Pension Service.

4. If you cannot afford premiums

Ask your municipality about payment consultation or reduction of National Health Insurance premiums. Ask Japan Pension Service about exemption or deferment of National Pension contributions. These are subject to application and screening.

This page provides general information, not legal advice. Procedures and eligibility vary by municipality, insurer and individual circumstances. Confirm current requirements with the relevant official office. Emergency information: follow local emergency instructions.
