Money, shopping and payment problems

Keep order records, contracts, payment records and official notices. Contact the relevant business or public office early.

An online order has not arrived

Check the order confirmation, delivery estimate and seller details. Contact the seller through the official platform. Save screenshots, emails and payment records. If you suspect fraud, contact your payment provider and police.

The product is defective or different from what I ordered

Take photos and keep the packaging, receipt and order details. Contact the seller promptly and ask about repair, replacement or refund options.

Debt repayments are difficult

Do not ignore payment notices or rely on high-fee debt-relief businesses. Consult a public debt counselling service, the Japan Legal Support Center (Houterasu), or the relevant lender association.

I cannot pay taxes or health insurance premiums

Contact the tax office or municipality as early as possible. Ask whether instalments, deferment or reductions may be available. Eligibility depends on the tax or premium and your circumstances.

This page provides general information, not legal advice. Procedures and eligibility vary by municipality, insurer and individual circumstances. Confirm current requirements with the relevant official office. Emergency information: follow local emergency instructions.
