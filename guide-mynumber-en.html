Lost your My Number Card?

If your card is lost or stolen, request temporary suspension immediately. Then report the loss and ask your municipality about reissue.

1. Suspend the card immediately

Call 0120-95-0178 and select option 2. The suspension service is available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Follow the official phone guidance.

2. Report the loss to the police

Visit a police station or police box and file a lost-property or theft report. Keep the report reference number if one is issued.

3. Ask your municipality about reissue

Contact the My Number Card desk at the municipality where you are registered. Ask which identity documents are required, whether an appointment is needed, and what fees apply. Requirements can vary.

If you find the card later

Do not assume it can be used again. Contact your municipality and follow its instructions for cancelling the suspension.
