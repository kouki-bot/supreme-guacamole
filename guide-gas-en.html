Smell gas? Take these steps immediately.

If you suspect a gas leak, prioritise safety. Do not try to find the leak yourself.

1. Do not create a spark

Do not light flames, smoke, or operate electrical switches, plugs, ventilation fans or appliances. Do not use your phone inside the affected area.

2. Ventilate by hand and leave

If it is safe, open doors and windows by hand. Do not operate powered ventilation. Evacuate the building and move away from the suspected leak.

3. Contact the gas emergency service from outside

Once outside and away from the suspected leak, contact your gas supplier's emergency number. If there is immediate danger, fire or injury, call 119. Follow the instructions of emergency personnel.
